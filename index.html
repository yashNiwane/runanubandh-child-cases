Child Safety Standards and CSAE Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

At Runanubandh, the safety and well-being of our users is our highest priority. We maintain a zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, trafficking, or any activity that endangers minors.

Age Restriction

Runanubandh is a matrimonial platform intended exclusively for adults who are 18 years of age or older.

Users under the age of 18 are not permitted to create accounts, access the platform, or use any of our services.

Prohibited Content and Behavior

The following activities are strictly prohibited:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)
  • Any content depicting, promoting, or facilitating the sexual exploitation of minors
  • Grooming or attempting to establish inappropriate relationships with minors
  • Human trafficking involving minors
  • Solicitation of sexual content involving minors
  • Sharing, storing, requesting, or distributing CSAE-related content
  • Any activity that violates child protection laws

Any account found engaging in such activities will be permanently removed.

Reporting Child Safety Concerns

Users can report profiles, content, or behavior that may violate these standards. Reports related to child safety are reviewed with priority and investigated promptly.

Enforcement Actions

Runanubandh may take one or more of the following actions:

  • Remove offending content
  • Suspend accounts
  • Permanently terminate accounts
  • Restrict platform access
  • Report violations to appropriate authorities where required by law

Cooperation with Law Enforcement

Runanubandh cooperates with law enforcement agencies and relevant authorities in the investigation of child sexual abuse, exploitation, trafficking, and other crimes involving minors, as required by applicable laws.

Commitment to Child Safety

We are committed to maintaining a safe platform and continuously improving our policies, moderation processes, and reporting mechanisms to prevent abuse and protect vulnerable individuals.
